How Acoustic Speaker Cleaning Works

CleanMySpeaker generates calibrated low-frequency sound waves (specifically 165 Hz sawtooth and square pulse waves) directly in your browser using the HTML5 Web Audio API. When played through smartphone micro-speakers, this frequency matches the natural mechanical resonance of the speaker cone, inducing maximum physical air displacement without dangerous volume clipping.

The resulting pneumatic air pulses break the capillary surface tension of liquid droplets trapped in the external speaker mesh, allowing water to escape onto an absorbent cloth.

Important Hardware Safety Guidelines

  • Never insert needles, safety pins, or toothpicks into speaker ports; doing so will puncture the waterproof acoustic membrane.
  • Never use high-pressure canned air or hair dryers, which can tear the speaker diaphragm and melt waterproof seals.
  • Keep device volume at a moderate 70% to 80% during audio cleaning cycles.
  • Do not place the speaker directly against your ear while playing high-frequency test sweeps.
