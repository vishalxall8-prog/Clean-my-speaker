How to Remove Water From a Phone Speaker Safely

If your phone speaker sounds muffled after your phone gets wet, water may have entered around the speaker area. The good news is that there are some simple steps you can try before seeking professional help.

Why Does a Phone Speaker Sound Muffled After Getting Wet?

A phone speaker needs to move air to produce sound. When moisture is present around the speaker openings, sound can become quieter, muffled, or distorted. Water can also collect around the speaker grille, which may temporarily affect sound quality.

What Should You Do First?

If your phone has recently been exposed to water:

  1. Disconnect the phone from charging.
  2. Remove any connected accessories.
  3. Wipe the outside of the phone with a soft, dry cloth.
  4. Keep the speaker opening facing downward when appropriate.
  5. Avoid putting the phone back on charge while there may still be moisture present.
  6. Give the device time to dry naturally.

Avoid using excessive heat or inserting sharp objects into the speaker grille.

Can Sound Help Remove Water From a Speaker?

A speaker produces vibrations when it plays audio. A suitable audio tone can create movement at the speaker and may help with small amounts of moisture around the speaker opening. However, a sound-based tool should not be considered a guaranteed way to remove water from every phone. If the device has significant water exposure or continues to malfunction, professional inspection may be necessary.

What Should You Avoid?

Avoid these common mistakes:

  • Do not insert pins, needles, or other sharp objects into the speaker.
  • Do not use excessive heat directly on the phone.
  • Do not keep charging a wet device.
  • Do not shake the phone aggressively.
  • Do not assume that a speaker-cleaning sound can repair internal water damage.

When Should You Get Professional Help?

If your phone continues to have problems after drying, professional assistance may be appropriate. Signs that deserve attention include persistent distorted audio, charging problems, unusual behavior, or other functions not working normally after significant water exposure.
